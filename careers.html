Senior Full Stack Data Engineer

Contact: dimitris@voreas.tech, tom@voreas.tech

Location: Remote / Atlanta

Employment Type: Full-time

Industry: Cybersecurity & Network Analytics

About Us

We are a cloud-native cybersecurity and network analytics company based in Atlanta, GA, focused on providing cutting-edge security insights through advanced data engineering. Our team is composed of experts in big data, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity, helping organizations make data-driven security decisions.With a fully remote, highly collaborative environment, we empower our engineers to innovate and solve complex challenges in cloud-scale cybersecurity analytics.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines and ETL processes using Spark (PySpark) and Python.
  • Architect and optimize data lakehouse and data warehouse solutions on AWS.
  • Develop APIs and integrations with security intelligence platforms.
  • Implement data governance, quality, and observability best practices.
  • Optimize performance of high-volume data processing systems.
  • Work closely with security teams to ensure compliance with cybersecurity regulations.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience as a Data Engineer, Full Stack Data Engineer, or similar role.
  • Strong expertise in Python, PySpark/Spark, and SQL.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS services like S3, Glue, Athena, EMR, Redshift, Lambda.
  • Experience with real-time streaming (Kafka, Kinesis, etc.).
  • Knowledge of data modeling, ETL frameworks, and performance tuning.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD, containerization (Docker), and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, CloudFormation).
  • Cybersecurity domain knowledge is a big plus.
  • U.S. citizenship is required (security clearance is a plus)

Why Join Us?

  • Fully remote with a modern, cloud-native environment.
  • Work on high-impact cybersecurity and network analytics projects.
  • Competitive salary and 401(k) with employer matching.
  • 15 days of paid time off (PTO) plus holidays.
  • Join a fast-growing team with room for leadership and innovation.

About VLI

VLI is a DARPA-funded technology spinoff from the Georgia Institute of Technology’s Center for Cyber Operations Enquiry and Unconventional Sensing (COEUS). VLI helps government agencies and systemically important organizations identify and track major nation state and criminal cyber actors across the Internet. VLI provides continuous, scalable, and oblivious-to-adversaries tracking of attack infrastructure affecting networks of interest.
